About Us

Named for the way we carry things

Kipkip is a Filipino word for carrying an item securely pressed against the side of the body, or under the arm. That is exactly the spirit of the bag: close, comfortable, and always with you.

We are a small partnership of six entrepreneurship students from San Jose del Monte, sewing and finishing each piece by hand. We aim to offer dependable, thoughtfully designed two-in-one bag pillows at accessible prices, while keeping fabric waste low and packaging simple.

Vision

A reputable, innovative local brand known for durable quality and dual-purpose convertible accessories for commuters, travelers, and students.

Mission

Space-saving everyday convenience with high production standards, accessible pricing, and ethical practice in our community.
